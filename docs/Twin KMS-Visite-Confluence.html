How do I safely restart the Oracle DB on RHEL 9 in CIB prod?

Follow the CIB-approved graceful sequence — never a hard kill on the instance:

  1. Notify the on-call DBA and raise a change ticket [1].
  2. Drain the listener, then shutdown immediate — confirm no active RMAN job is running [1].
  3. On RHEL 9, watch for PGA OOM on large-pages hosts; the 2026-04 cascade started here [2].
  4. Restart, then validate AWR snapshots before re-opening to traffic [3].
Tip — the runbook ships a pre-flight checklist script; run it first to confirm the listener drain completed cleanly.
