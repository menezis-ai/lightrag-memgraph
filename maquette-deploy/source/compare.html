Twin RAG vs Kore.ai

Knowledge management positioning · meeting recap 2026-05-21 → Manu / Vihn / Julien

"Your platform is focused on how to create agents efficiently. Our need is before this step. It's knowledge management. Your target is not aligned with our need right now." — Emmanuel (Manu) to Kore.ai (Theo · Amar), 2026-05-21

Kore.ai — "AI for agents"

Search AI + AI for Process + AI for Work — three modules to mix-and-match. Knowledge management is a side-concern (weighting + ingestion connectors). Audit, governance, MyAccess inheritance are gaps Manu surfaced — Kore.ai had no shipped answer, asked to "come back later".

Twin RAG — BNP-native hub KMS

Unifies what BNP already has into one operator console. Two complementary RAG tiers under one UI : RAG 1.5 (Elasticsearch · nightly Confluence + SharePoint ingest via Crosspoint, breadth) and Twin's own LightRAG + Memgraph Twincore patch v0.5.3 (graph reasoning, depth, steward governance). SSO BNPP for identity, MyAccess for entity-level ITGP heritance. Steward review + visible audit trail designed-in. Knowledge is the product; agents are downstream consumers.
